Why FurQan?

The Criterion

FurQan means the criterion — the guidance that separates truth from falsehood, right from wrong, and light from darkness. Quran FurQan is built to help you read, understand and live by that clear distinction, verse by verse.

Truth from falsehood

Clear translation and explanation, without confusion or guesswork.

Right from wrong

Guidance you can follow in everyday life, in plain English.

Light from darkness

A calm, readable text that brings clarity to every surah.

Arabic and English

Keep the original Arabic beside the English, so you never lose the source.

Tafsir

Understand every verse with classic tafsir

Quran FurQan includes 2 classic tafsirs to explain the meaning simply and reliably: Tafsir al-Muyassar, for a clear, accessible explanation, and Tafsir al-Saadi, for a deeper, trusted commentary.

Muyassar

A simplified explanation that makes the meaning easy to follow, even if you are new to the Qur'an.

Saadi

A respected classical commentary that adds depth, context and reflection to your reading.
