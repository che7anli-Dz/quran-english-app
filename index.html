What is Al-Furqan?

“FurQan” in the app's name is not decoration. Al-Furqan is one of the Qur'an's own names for itself — the standard by which things are judged and told apart.

ٱلْفُرْقَان
Al-Furqān

From the Arabic root f-r-q: to separate, to distinguish clearly. It is also the name of the Qur'an's 25th chapter.

Truth from falsehood — claims are weighed against a clear standard, not left to guesswork.

Right from wrong — guidance for how to live, stated plainly enough to act on.

Light from darkness — the Qur'an describes itself as light brought to people who were in the dark about God.

الله

“Allah” — a word worth pausing on

Allah is simply the Arabic word for God — the one God of Abraham, Moses, and Jesus. Arab Christians and Jews use the same word in their own scriptures and prayers. In the Qur'an it is not a different deity; it is the same God, addressed in the language the Qur'an was revealed in. The app's glossary explains words like this wherever they first appear.
